NO CAP

the unofficial sport of the lunch table

Water bottle flip. Swipe up, it flips once, it lands upright. You know the game. This is that game, same bottles, same cafeteria table, in your browser.

1
Swipe up to flip. Power and angle are yours; the physics is real.
2
Land it upright, that's a point. On the cap? That's 1,000 aura and everyone at the table saw it.
3
Nine kids sit between you and the 40 oz. Beat a kid, keep their bottle. Beat the table, keep the 40 oz.

Every game is recorded by default, two ways. A video of the table (1080×1920 vertical, with sound, ready for Shorts) records on this device while you play; a red REC timer shows it running, and the end card has a Save button. A tape (every flip, replayable) is saved to the shared shelf below so anyone with the link can watch it back. Clip mode adds the countdown and hides the buttons for screen recordings.
